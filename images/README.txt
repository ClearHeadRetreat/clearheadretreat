How to shoot them
-----------------
Landscape 4:3, around 1600x1200. Curtains open, lights on, beds made,
benches clear. Real and slightly imperfect beats polished stock - that is
the whole point of using your own photos.

Compression
-----------
Keep JPEGs under about 400 KB. Cap the long edge at 1600px, quality 84,
progressive. Anything bigger slows the page down and costs you SEO.
