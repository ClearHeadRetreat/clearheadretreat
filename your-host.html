Why this exists

The short version

Wade started Clear Head Retreat because he knows what it's like to run flat out for years and slowly lose track of your own life. Nothing dramatic, just too much noise and no room to hear yourself over it.

What pulled him back was nothing clever. Time outside, a bit of hard work he actually chose, and enough quiet to think straight.

When he went looking for a place that did that for other men, everything he found was either clinical, expensive, or a hall full of people being told to open up on cue. So he built the thing he wishes had existed. Turangi, because the hills and the river do half the work. Three at a time, because you can't hide in a group that size. And no fixed programme, because what one man needs from time off is rarely what the next one needs.

It isn't only for men who are struggling, either. Some come because they're close to burnt out. Others come because three mates wanted a few days in the bush with someone who knows the place and does the organising. Both are welcome, and they often end up on the same track.

Behind the scenes

Not a one-man band

Wade is the one you'll spend the days with, but he isn't running the place on his own. Celeste is part of the team behind Clear Head Retreat and has a hand in how it's run.

That's worth saying plainly, because a men's retreat run by one man out of a house he owns is a fair thing to be cautious about. This is a small family business with more than one person accountable for it, real names, photos of the actual house, and a proper conversation before anyone commits to anything.

The specialist activities are run by local guides and operators who have been doing this here for years.

How it's held

A few things we believe

Quiet is useful

Most men don't need more advice. They need room to hear what they already know.

Honesty over performance

Nobody has to have it together here. The whole point is you don't.

Getting outside helps

A day on the tracks or the water shifts something a meeting room never could.

You set the pace

Do all of it or none of it. Nobody's counting, and nobody's pushed.

Small and personal

It's a small group, so nobody is managing a crowd. Wade is simply there with you.

What's said here, stays here

Discretion isn't a policy line for us. It's the whole foundation.

Being straight with you

What this isn't

Wade isn't a therapist or a counsellor, and Clear Head Retreat isn't treatment. It's a considered, facilitated experience for men who are basically okay but overloaded, and for men who simply want time outdoors in decent company.

If you're in real crisis, it wouldn't be fair to pretend a few days away is the answer, and part of the short conversation before you come is both sides being honest about whether this is the right fit. If it isn't, we'll happily point you somewhere that is.

In New Zealand you can call or text 1737 any time to talk with a trained counsellor, or contact your GP for support.
