What this is

A small retreat. Not a programme, not a course.

Clear Head Retreat is a private stay at a quiet home in Turangi.

Time outside, good food, and no rush. It has a shape and someone runs it, but it isn't therapy or counselling. It's a break, and it's the sort most of us keep putting off.

No two run the same. We set the dates, the length and the pace around whoever is coming.

You don't need a reason beyond wanting the time away. Most people come because they fancy a few days outdoors with good company.

Honest about fit

Who it's for, and who it isn't.

This is for you if you…

  • Want a few days outdoors with good company
  • Like the sound of a quiet house and no schedule
  • Value your privacy
  • Want time to think without anyone hurrying you
  • Feel overloaded and need a reset

It isn't the right place if you…

  • Are in a mental health crisis
  • Are looking for therapy or counselling
  • Are after a party weekend or a packed itinerary

If you're in crisis, please reach out to a GP or, in New Zealand, call or text 1737 any time to talk with a trained counsellor.

Who's behind it

Meet Wade.

Wade was born in Rhodesia, now Zimbabwe, and grew up in South Africa. He lived in the UK for a number of years before settling in New Zealand. He has been self-employed most of his working life. He's been married for over thirty years and has two sons.

He runs. Marathons and ultramarathons, and most days a track somewhere. Being outdoors is how he clears his own head. That's a fair part of why the retreat works the way it does.

He received a commendation and a bravery award during his years in the UK. Closer to home, he's the one family call when things get hard. Helping people has always come naturally to him.

Getting outside

Plenty to do. None of it compulsory.

There's no set schedule. These are things you can do, not things you have to do. Some people are out from morning till dark, others are happy with a coffee and a book.

Some depend on the weather and the season. Specialist activities are run by local guides we know and trust. If there's something else you'd like to do, just ask.

A few quiet days in good country.

Group size

Three at a time, no more.

We keep it small on purpose. Nobody sits at the back and nobody gets treated like a delegate.

Wade gets to know you properly, and that only works with a few of you.

Come on your own and we'll place you with an open retreat. Or bring two others you already know and have the house to yourselves, whether that's friends, workmates or family.

  • It's easy to relax in a group this size
  • The house stays quiet and easy
  • People tend to stay in touch afterwards

By invitation

There's no booking button, and that's on purpose.

Every retreat is arranged personally. You send a short enquiry, we give you a call, and we work out the dates, the length and the cost from there.
